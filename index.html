FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with international clients?

Yes — I work remotely with clients worldwide and keep overlap hours for the US, UK, and EU.

How long does a website take?

Most sites take about 1–3 weeks, depending on scope and how quickly feedback comes back.

Should I choose React or WordPress?

Choose WordPress when you need editable content and a CMS out of the box. Choose React when you want a custom app, dashboard, or headless storefront with a more interactive UI.

Can you integrate an API into my existing site?

Yes — I can connect REST APIs and third-party services into your existing site.

Yes — every project includes post-launch support so you're not left guessing after go-live.
