Overview

A high-motion marketing site for Kynetix, an influencer marketing agency connecting brands and creators. Built to feel cinematic — with scroll storytelling, roster showcases, and clear paths for both brands and creators to get in touch.

The Challenge

The agency needed a site that matched the energy of creator culture without sacrificing speed or clarity — room for motion, case work, and dual CTAs for brands and creators.

What I Built

  • Next.js app with Tailwind CSS and GSAP scroll animations
  • Brand × creator partnership showcase and roster sections
  • Contact flows for brands and creators
  • Responsive layout tuned for desktop and mobile storytelling

Tech Stack

  • React
  • Next.js
  • Tailwind CSS
  • GSAP

Results

  • Live agency site at thekynetix.com
  • Scroll-driven experience that supports brand and creator acquisition
