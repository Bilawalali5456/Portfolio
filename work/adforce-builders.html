Case study

WordPress Website

Adforce Builders.

Construction & architecture website · WordPress

Visit live site All projects

Overview

Adforce Builders is a construction and architecture firm in Pakistan that designs and builds homes on every common plot size. They needed a website that presents their services clearly, builds trust with homeowners making one of the biggest purchases of their lives, and turns visitors into enquiries.

The Challenge

House buyers in Pakistan think in plot sizes: 5 Marla, 8 Marla, 1 Kanal, 2 Kanal. Each size comes with different layouts, budgets and expectations. The firm's offering had to be organized the way customers actually search, so a visitor could find the design option for their plot in a few clicks, understand what is included, and contact the team without friction. The site also had to look credible and load quickly on mobile, where most visitors browse.

What I Built

  • Service pages organized by plot size, from 8 Marla to 2 Kanal, so each visitor lands on the option that fits their plot.
  • A consistent page structure for every service: overview, what's included, and a clear call-to-action to get in touch.
  • A clean, trust-focused homepage that introduces the firm, its services and its process.
  • Contact calls-to-action placed throughout the site so enquiries are never more than one scroll away.
  • A fully responsive, mobile-first layout tested across devices and browsers.
  • Speed and on-page SEO basics: clean headings, optimized images and descriptive page titles for each service.

Tech Stack

  • WordPress
  • Custom Theme Setup
  • Responsive Design
  • On-page SEO
  • Speed Optimization

Results

The site is live and gives Adforce Builders a professional home online. Services are now organized the way homeowners search, each plot size has its own page that can rank on Google, and the team can add new house designs without touching code.
